# The bar

The bar is a slim strip at the right edge of the window. It holds the things you only need to glance
at now and then: suggestions, what agents need from you, what you captured, and Ask. It is always
there, in the Mac app and in a browser.

## What is in the bar

Four icons sit at the top of the strip and one at the bottom. Point at an icon to see its name and key.

| Icon | What it shows | Key |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Suggestions** | What tail offers for the page in front. See [Tail](/ai/tail). | <kbd>⌥⌘S</kbd> |
| **Feed** | What agents need from you, such as a question to answer. See [Feed and feedback](/ai/feed-feedback). | <kbd>⌥⌘F</kbd> |
| **Captures** | What you captured, waiting to be reviewed. See [The capture inbox](/capture/inbox). | <kbd>⌥⌘K</kbd> |
| **Ask** | The thread with Claude about the page, and its composer. See [Ask Claude](/ai/ask). | <kbd>⌘J</kbd> |
| **Quick capture** (at the bottom) | Opens the capture box for an idea, a web address or a voice note. See [Quick capture](/capture/quick-capture). | <kbd>⌃⌥I</kbd> |

## Open and close a panel

Click an icon to open its panel beside the strip. The page column narrows to make room; the panel
never covers your words. Click the same icon again, or press its key again, to close the panel and give
the room back to the pages.

Clicking another icon while a panel is open switches the panel to that view. Quick capture is
different: it opens the capture box rather than a panel.

To make the panel wider or narrower, drag its left edge. It keeps that width.

## Counts

A small number on an icon tells you something is waiting there. It shows only when there is something
to see, so the strip stays quiet the rest of the time.

- **Feed** counts the questions and requests from agents you have not answered yet.
- **Captures** counts captures you have not reviewed yet.
- **Suggestions** counts suggestions you have not seen yet. Opening the panel marks them as seen.

Ask and Quick capture never show a count. When you answer a question or mark a capture reviewed, its
count goes down, and it goes away when nothing is left.

## The panel follows the page

When a page that has suggestions comes to the front, an open panel switches to **Suggestions** for
that page. Nothing else switches the panel.

To keep the panel on what it shows, click the pin in the panel's header. While it is pinned, opening
a page with suggestions leaves the panel where it is, and the Suggestions count tells you what waits.
Click the pin again to unpin it.

## Ask in the bar

When you select words and ask about them, or press <kbd>⌘J</kbd>, the panel opens on **Ask** with the
page's thread. The ask bar beside the selected words works as before. See [Ask Claude](/ai/ask).

## Captures in the bar

**Captures** lists the same things as the capture inbox, newest first, with the same review ticks.
Opening an item there opens its page beside the page you are on. See
[The capture inbox](/capture/inbox).

## In a narrow window

When the window is narrow, a panel opens as a card beside the page instead of a column beside the
strip. The card is titled with the panel's name: **Suggestions**, **Feed**, **Captures** or **Ask**.
The strip stays at the right edge.

::: info Limits
In a narrow window the panel has no pin, and it does not switch to Suggestions when a page with
suggestions comes to the front.
:::

## Keys

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| <kbd>⌥⌘S</kbd> | Show or hide Suggestions |
| <kbd>⌥⌘F</kbd> | Show or hide the Feed |
| <kbd>⌥⌘K</kbd> | Show or hide Captures |
| <kbd>⌘J</kbd> | Ask about the selected words, or the page |
| <kbd>⌃⌥I</kbd> | Quick capture |

You can change any of these in **Settings › Shortcuts**.
