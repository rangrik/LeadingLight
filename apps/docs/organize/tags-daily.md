# Tags and daily notes

Tags gather pages on one topic. Daily notes give each day a page of its own, and show what you made that day. Both are ordinary pages with a type, so they work with links, lists and properties like any other page.

## Tag a page

There are three ways to tag a page:

- **From the header.** Hover above the title of a plain page and click **Add tag**. Type a tag's name and pick it, or pick **Create** to make a new one. The page gets a **Tags** row holding it.
- **From the Tags row.** Most starter types, such as Book and Person, already have a **Tags** property. Click its value to add tags. See [Properties](/organize/properties).
- **In the text.** Type <kbd>#</kbd> and a word, such as "#reading", and pick the tag, or **Create #"reading"**. The tag links that line.

A tag is a page of the type Tag. Each new tag is listed under **Tags** in the sidebar's Types. Tags don't appear in Recent.

The slash menu can make a tag too: type "/tag", press <kbd>Return</kbd> and type its name. See [Tasks and new objects](/organize/tasks).

## See what is tagged

Open a tag, from **Tags** in the sidebar or by clicking a tag anywhere. On its page, **Tagged** lists every page with that tag, with a count, such as **Tagged · 2**. You can show them as a list, table, gallery or board, sorted and grouped. See [Lists, tables and boards](/organize/views).

A tag is a page, so you can also write in it, give it an icon, and see its backlinks.

## Merge two tags

If you have two tags for one topic, such as "scifi" and "sci-fi", merge them.

1. Open the tag you want to remove, such as "scifi".
2. Click **Merge into…**. It says *Its links move to the tag you pick; then it is deleted.*
3. Pick the tag to keep, such as "sci-fi".

The first tag is gone and the one you kept opens, listing the pages from both. "#scifi" in your text now reads "#sci-fi".

## Today's note

Click **Today** at the top of the sidebar. A page titled with today's full date opens, of the type Daily note. There is one note per day: click **Today** again and the same page opens.

<img class="shot" src="/screenshots/today.webp" alt="Today’s daily note, with everything made that day listed below" />

At the top of a daily note are:

| Button | Opens |
| --- | --- |
| **Previous day** | The day before. |
| **Today** | Today's note. Greyed out while you are on it. |
| **Next day** | The day after. |
| **Pick a day** | A calendar, to jump to any day. |

Each day's note opens titled with its date.

### Days are kept only once you write

Browsing days leaves nothing behind. A day's note is stored the first time you edit it; until then it isn't in **Daily notes**, even after a reload. So you can step back a week and forward again without making seven empty notes.

Linking a day keeps it at once. Type "@today" in any page to link today's note; if it wasn't stored yet, it is now. See [Links and backlinks](/organize/links).

Daily notes stay out of Recent. Find them all under **Daily notes** in the sidebar's Types, newest first.

## What was made today

At the end of a daily note, **Created this day** lists the pages you made that day, with a count, such as **Created this day · 2**. Click one to open it.

- Tags and other daily notes aren't listed; they have their own places.
- A page the note already links in its body isn't listed again.
- The list updates while the note is open, without a reload.

### Ideas show in their day

An idea you capture with <kbd>⌃⌥I</kbd> is an Idea page, so it shows in **Created this day** on the day you had it. The note's body isn't touched: nothing is added to what you wrote. Ideas you make by hand show in their day the same way. See [Idea capture](/capture/idea-capture).

## A template for daily notes

To start every day with the same headings or checklist, give the Daily note type a default template.

1. Open **Daily notes** in the sidebar and click **Type settings**.
2. Click **New template** and write what each day should start with.
3. Back in **Type settings**, click the star beside the template to make it the default, and **Save**.

When you open a day that has no note yet, its note starts from the template. As with any day, it is stored only once you edit it. See [Types and templates](/organize/types).
