# History and authorship

LeadingLight keeps a history of every page as you work, on your device. You never manage versions: go back to any earlier moment when you need it, name a moment worth keeping, and see which words you wrote and which an agent added.

## Open a page's history

Click the clock button in the page's card header (**History and authorship**). Its menu has:

| Item | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Page history** | Opens the list of the page's versions. |
| **Save milestone…** | Opens the history, ready to name this moment. |
| **Show authorship** | Tints words by who added them. A tick shows it is on. |
| **Inspect authorship** | Lists the page's words by who added them. |

## How history is kept

Changes save on their own as you type. History groups your typing into editing sessions of about five minutes, so the list reads as moments rather than every keystroke. Each version shows who made it (**You**, or an agent and its model) and when.

The oldest row, **Before history began**, holds the page as it was before history was kept for it.

Pick a version to see it:

- **Preview** shows the page as it was, read only.
- **Changes** shows the words **Before** and **After**, and a title change if there was one. If the version also changed properties, it says so.

## Save a milestone

A milestone is a named moment, such as "Before review", that you can find again easily.

1. Choose **Save milestone…** in the history menu, or click **Save milestone** at the top of **Page history**.
2. Type a name of up to 120 characters.
3. Click **Save milestone**.

The milestone shows in the list by its name, and keeps its own place: editing after it starts a new session. It is kept after you quit and reopen the app.

## Restore a version

1. Open **Page history** and pick the version.
2. Click **Restore this version**.

The page goes back to that version's title, body and properties. Restoring adds a new version on top; it doesn't erase anything. The version from before the restore stays in history, so you can always restore it in turn.

To keep an old version without changing the page, click **Keep a copy**. It opens as a new page, titled with "(copy)".

An agent's report stays as it was published, so it can't be restored to another version. Keep a copy if you want to edit one.

## Undo an agent's action

When an agent works on your notes, everything it did in one turn is one action, even if it changed several pages. For example, it might make a task and link it from your note.

1. Open **Page history** on a page the agent changed and pick its version.
2. Click **Undo entire action**.

Every change in that action is undone together, in one saved change: the task and the link both go. The action and its undo both stay in history.

Undo never throws away your later work. If one of those pages changed after the agent's action, or you edited it between two of the agent's steps, the app says *There are newer changes on a page in this action. Nothing was changed.* and no page changes. Restore a version or keep a copy instead.

## Your work is safe while it saves

- **A failed save is not hidden.** If your device refuses a save, the page keeps your unsaved edits and tells you. No version is recorded for it. In **Page history** you can click **Keep my unsaved edits as a new page**.
- **Two tabs can't overwrite each other.** If the same page is open in two browser tabs and both save, the older save is refused rather than replacing the newer page, and its text stays available.
- **A sudden close keeps your last edit.** If Safari reloads or closes in the middle of a save, the last edit is recovered, and a newer version is kept separately.
- **Deleting right after typing keeps the words.** If you delete a page before its last words are saved, the deletion still records them in history.

## See who wrote what

LeadingLight records who added words as they enter the page: you, or an agent. This is a record of where words came from in LeadingLight, not a guess about whether text was written by AI.

### Show authorship

Choose **Show authorship** in the history menu. Words an agent added are tinted, so you can tell them from your own at a glance. Choose it again to turn it off. Turning it on or off never changes the page or its history.

If you edit an agent's words, your changes are yours and the words you left are still the agent's. Restoring a milestone or undoing typing keeps each word's recorded author; the restore itself is recorded as yours. Who wrote a word shows by touch as well as with the mouse.

### Inspect authorship

Choose **Inspect authorship** to open **Authorship**, which lists the page's words in runs, each under its author:

| Author | Means |
| --- | --- |
| **You** | Words you typed or pasted in LeadingLight. |
| An agent, with its model | Words an agent added. |
| **Origin unknown** | Words from before authorship was recorded. |

Text you paste is marked as added by you. LeadingLight doesn't know who first wrote it in another app, and doesn't claim to.

::: tip
A selected recording block is protected: typing while it is selected doesn't change the recording or its transcript. Click into the transcript to edit it, and your words there are marked as yours. See [Voice notes](/capture/voice).
:::
