# Icons, covers and descriptions

The top of every page can carry an icon, a cover and a one-line description. All three are optional. They make a page easy to spot in the sidebar, in lists and on cards.

<img class="shot" src="/screenshots/page-header.webp" alt="A Book page with an orange cover, a desert emoji icon and a description under the title" />

## Show the header controls

Move the pointer over the top of a page. **Add icon**, **Add cover** and **Add description** appear just above the title, without moving it. They also show while one of them has keyboard focus. On a plain page you may see **Add tag** there too (see [Tags and daily notes](/organize/tags-daily)).

Each control goes away once its part is set. In a narrow page they shrink to icons, and still fit inside the page.

## Add an icon

Click **Add icon**. The **Icon** picker opens with three tabs: **Emoji**, **Icons** and **Upload**.

<img class="shot" src="/screenshots/icon-picker.webp" alt="The icon picker with Emoji, Icons and Upload tabs" />

The icon shows large above the title, and small in the sidebar row and the card header, in place of the page symbol.

### An emoji

The **Emoji** tab opens with the search box ready. Every emoji is listed in groups, starting with *Smileys & Emotion*.

- Type to search. Search matches more than the name: "happy" finds *grinning face*. If nothing matches, the picker says **No results**.
- Click an emoji to pick it. The picker closes.
- You can also type or paste an emoji straight into the search box, and it becomes the icon.

The **Emoji** block in the editor uses the same full set of emoji.

### An icon in a colour

1. Open the **Icons** tab.
2. Pick a colour, such as **Blue**.
3. Type to find an icon, such as "book", and click it.

The icon shows in the colour you picked. The picker remembers the colour the next time you open it.

### Your own image

Open the **Upload** tab and choose an image file, or drop one on it. It shows as a square with rounded corners above the title, and small in the sidebar. A file that isn't an image is refused with **Choose an image file**.

### Change or remove the icon

Click the icon above the title (**Change icon**) to open the picker again and pick another. To remove it, click **Remove** in the picker. The sidebar shows the page symbol again.

## Add a cover

Click **Add cover**. The **Cover** picker has four tabs: **Colour**, **Unsplash**, **Upload** and **Link**.

<img class="shot" src="/screenshots/cover-picker.webp" alt="The cover picker with Colour, Unsplash, Upload and Link tabs" />

A cover is a band across the whole width of the page, above the title. If the page has an icon, the icon sits on the cover's lower edge.

| Tab | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| **Colour** | Click a colour, such as **Green**, for a plain band. |
| **Unsplash** | Search Unsplash's free photos and click one. See below. |
| **Upload** | Choose or paste an image. It fills the band, cropped to fit. |
| **Link** | Paste an image's address and press <kbd>Return</kbd>. If the address doesn't load as an image, the picker says **That link didn't load as an image** and the cover stays as it was. |

### A photo from Unsplash

1. Open the **Unsplash** tab. The **Search Unsplash** box is ready; you don't need an Unsplash account or a key.
2. Type what you want, such as "mountain". Up to 24 landscape photos show in a grid, each named **Photo by** and the photographer.
3. Click a photo. It fills the cover and the picker closes.

Unsplash asks that photographers are credited. Hover the cover and its top right reads **Photo by Ann Lee on Unsplash**: the name opens the photographer's Unsplash profile, and "Unsplash" opens unsplash.com. In a narrow page a long name is cut short with "…"; hover it to see it in full. The credit goes away if you change the cover to something else.

If a search doesn't work, the tab says why:

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **No photos found** | Nothing matched. Try other words. |
| **Couldn't reach Unsplash** | You are offline, or Unsplash can't be reached. |
| **Unsplash's hourly limit is used up. Try again later.** | The shared search has been used too much this hour. |
| **Photo search is temporarily unavailable. Try again later.** | The image service isn't working right now. |

The cover never changes because of a failed search.

::: tip What Unsplash search sends
Photo search goes through a small shared image service, so you don't need your own Unsplash key. It receives only the words you search for and which photo you pick, which Unsplash asks to be told. It never receives your notes. The photos themselves load straight from Unsplash.
:::

### Unsplash covers offline

An Unsplash photo loads from the internet. When it can't load, the cover is a plain grey band with no broken-image sign, and the credit still shows on hover. The photo comes back once you are online again, or when you pick it again.

### Reposition a cover

When an image is taller than the band, you can choose which part shows.

1. Hover the cover and click **Reposition**. The cover says **Drag to reposition**.
2. Drag the image up or down.
3. Click **Save position**, or **Cancel** to put it back where it was.

The position is kept, also for Unsplash photos.

### Change or remove the cover

Hover the cover and click **Change cover** to pick another. To remove it, click **Remove** in the picker. The title moves back up to where it started.

## Add a description

A description is one line under the title that says what the page is.

1. Click **Add description**. A field shows under the title with the placeholder *Add a description*.
2. Type the line and press <kbd>Return</kbd>. The cursor moves to the body.

Clear the field and click elsewhere to remove it; **Add description** comes back. In the **Add property** list, **Description** is offered too, marked *Under the title*: it is this same line, not a second property.

Gallery cards show a page's description, or else the first lines of its body. See [Lists, tables and boards](/organize/views).

## Pages side by side

With two pages open side by side, each keeps its own icon, cover and description. Each cover spans only its own page, and each card header shows its own page's icon.
