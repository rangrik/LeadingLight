# Pages and the sidebar

Everything you write in LeadingLight is a page. The sidebar lists your pages so you can get back to them, and each page has its own title, body and options.

## Make a page

Click **New page** at the right of the **Recent** label in the sidebar. When you have no pages yet, the main area shows **No page open** with a **New page** button too.

The new page opens with the cursor in its title. The body below shows *Type '/' for commands* until you start writing. Pages save as you type; there is nothing to confirm.

## Give it a title

Type in the title field at the top of the page. The sidebar row and the card header change as you type.

- Press <kbd>Return</kbd> in the title to move to the body. The title stays on one line.
- A long title wraps onto more lines on the page. In the sidebar it stays on one line and ends with "…".
- A page with no title, or a title of only spaces, is called **Untitled**.

The card header above the page shows where you are: the type's plural name, then the page, as in **Pages › Trip to Kyoto**. Click the type's name to open the list of all pages of that type in the same card. <kbd>⇧</kbd>-click it to open the list beside the page instead.

## Find pages in Recent

**Recent** lists your pages, newest first. Daily notes, tags, tasks and templates live in their own lists under **Types**, not in Recent. A page you open shows as the current row.

To change the order, click **Sort Recent** beside **New page** and pick one:

| Sort by | Order |
| --- | --- |
| **Created** | The newest page you made comes first. This is the default. |
| **Updated** | The page you last edited comes first. Opening a page without changing it doesn't move it. |

Your choice is kept after you close the app.

## Pin a page

Pinned pages sit in their own **Pinned** group above **Recent**, so the ones you use every day stay in reach.

1. Hover a row in the sidebar and click **More** (the "…" button).
2. Choose **Pin**.

The page moves from Recent into **Pinned**. To take it out, choose **Unpin** in the same menu. When no page is pinned, the Pinned group goes away.

## The row menu

Hover any row in the sidebar and click **More** to see:

| Item | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Rename** | Opens a field under the row with the whole name selected. Type a new name and press <kbd>Return</kbd>, or click elsewhere. <kbd>Esc</kbd> keeps the old name, and so does an empty field. |
| **Pin** / **Unpin** | Moves the page into or out of **Pinned**. |
| **Copy link** | Copies a link to the page. A toast reads **Link copied**. Paste it into another page's body and it becomes a link that opens the page and follows its name if you rename it. |
| **Delete** | Asks first, then deletes the page. |

## Delete a page

1. Choose **Delete** in the row's menu, or **Delete page…** at the end of the page's **Page options** menu.
2. A dialog asks **Delete "Draft"?** and says the page and everything in it will be removed.
3. Click **Delete** to confirm, or **Cancel** to keep the page.

After a delete, the next page in the list opens. If it was your last page, the sidebar shows **No pages yet**.

Links to a deleted page from other pages read **Deleted page**, struck through. See [Links and backlinks](/organize/links).

::: info Limits
There is no trash view yet. A deleted page is removed from your lists at once.
:::

## Thoughts and essays under a page

A page's sidenotes are kept on its **Thoughts** page, and an essay you start from it is a page of its own. Both belong to their page, so the sidebar doesn't list them at the top level.

When you open the page, its Thoughts page and essay show under its row as a small tree. The branch to the one you are on is drawn in the theme's main colour. Open another page and the tree folds away. Their row menus offer only **Copy link** and **Delete**.

If you delete the page itself, its Thoughts page and essay stay, and are listed at the top level from then on.

To start an essay, open **Page options** and choose **Start an essay on this page**. Once there is one, the item reads **Open essay**. See [Sidenotes, thoughts and essays](/write/sidenotes).

## Page options

The **Page options** button ("…") sits in the page's property bar, the line under the title with the **Type** picker and **Add property**. It holds the page's own actions:

- **Save as type…** turns the page's properties into a new type. See [Types and templates](/organize/types).
- **Start an essay on this page** (or **Open essay**).
- **To-do and highlight style**, with **Plain** and **Playful**. See below.
- **Delete page…**, in red.

To open the list of all pages of this type, click the type's name in the card header.

## Page styles: Plain and Playful

Each page has a style. It changes how to-dos and highlights look, so a page with neither looks the same in both.

| Style | Checked to-dos | Highlights |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Plain** (the default) | A filled box with a check. The words are muted and struck through with a straight line. No sound. | A plain highlight. |
| **Playful** | An open box with a hand-drawn check and a hand-drawn line through the words. A to-do you check now is drawn off like a pen, with a soft tap. | A marker stroke. |

To change it, open **Page options** and pick **Plain** or **Playful** under **To-do and highlight style**. The menu closes and nothing on the page moves. Each page keeps its own style.

::: info Limits
Task lines on a Playful page still show a plain box and a straight line, not the pen marks.
:::
