# Pages and the sidebar

Everything you write in LeadingLight is a page. The sidebar keeps pinned pages in reach and Search library holds the rest, and each page has its own title, body and options.

## Make a page

Click **New page** near the top of the Notes sidebar, or press <kbd>⌘N</kbd>. When you have no pages yet, the main area shows **No page open** with a **New page** button too.

The new page opens in a new tab with the cursor in its title. Your previous tab stays open. In a browser pane, <kbd>⌘N</kbd> opens the address picker for a new browser tab. The body below shows *Type '/' for commands* until you start writing. Pages save as you type; there is nothing to confirm.

Use the arrow beside **New page** to choose any type, including your own types. You can also press <kbd>⌘K</kbd> and type **new meeting** or **new book**. The new page uses that type's default template. **New daily note** opens today's note.

## Give it a title

Type in the title field at the top of the page. The sidebar row and the card header change as you type.

- Press <kbd>Return</kbd> in the title to move to the body. The title stays on one line.
- A long title wraps onto more lines on the page. In the sidebar it stays on one line and ends with "…".
- A page with no title, or a title of only spaces, is called **Untitled**.

The card header above the page shows where you are: the type's plural name, then the page, as in **Pages › Trip to Kyoto**. Click the type's name to open the list of all pages of that type in the same card. <kbd>⇧</kbd>-click it to open the list beside the page instead.

## Find pages in Library

Press <kbd>⌘K</kbd> or choose **Search library** in the sidebar. The same search works with the sidebar hidden. On Mac, **All** opens with three actions under **Create**. **Idea** is selected, followed by **Page** and **Browser tab**. Press <kbd>Return</kbd> to open the selection in a new tab in the focused pane, or <kbd>⇧Return</kbd> to open it beside that pane. Recent pages follow in order of their last change. The **Pages** category lists every saved page, including daily notes and unpinned pages.

Search by title, words in the body, or type. A search for **book** offers matching books, **Books** to open that type's list, and **New book**. **Types** lists all your types. **Tabs** shows open websites when the browser is available. Use <kbd>Tab</kbd> and <kbd>⇧Tab</kbd> to cycle through categories, or <kbd>⌘1</kbd>–<kbd>⌘5</kbd> to jump directly. Your search stays in the field. Templates stay in their type's settings.

If your search finds nothing, press Return on **New page “…”** to use your search as the title. To choose a type, click **Create**. Its icons are grouped into Writing, Planning, Reference and My types. If you already typed a title, Create lets you use it for a new page even when other pages matched your search. You can also type **new meeting Friday review** in **All** and press Return to start a Meeting with that title. Your custom types and their default templates work the same way. **New daily note** opens today’s note.

While search is open, <kbd>⌘1</kbd> selects All, <kbd>⌘2</kbd> Create, <kbd>⌘3</kbd> Pages, <kbd>⌘4</kbd> Types and <kbd>⌘5</kbd> Tabs when available.

Large lists show 100 pages at a time. **Next pages** and **Previous 100 pages** move through the results. Typing always searches the whole library.

Open a browser-tab result to return to that website, or a note result to return to Notes. In a mixed split, the other pane stays open. Use **Open beside**, <kbd>⇧Return</kbd> or Shift-click to open a result beside the current pane. <kbd>⌘</kbd>-click opens a note in a new tab. <kbd>Esc</kbd> closes search and returns focus. After opening a result, press <kbd>⌘K</kbd> again to type a new search immediately.

## Pin a page

Pinned pages sit in their own **Pinned** group in the Notes sidebar, so the ones you use every day stay in reach.

1. Find the page in **Search library** and highlight it with the arrow keys or pointer.
2. Choose **Pin** at the foot of search.

The page appears in **Pinned** and remains in Library. To take it out, choose **Unpin** in search or in the sidebar row's menu. When no page is pinned, a short hint explains where to find pages.

## The row menu

Hover any row in the sidebar and click **More** to see:

| Item | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Open beside** | Opens the page beside the current pane. |
| **Rename** | Opens a field under the row with the whole name selected. Type a new name and press <kbd>Return</kbd>, or click elsewhere. <kbd>Esc</kbd> keeps the old name, and so does an empty field. |
| **Pin** / **Unpin** | Moves the page into or out of **Pinned**. |
| **Copy link** | Copies a link to the page. A toast reads **Link copied**. Paste it into another page's body and it becomes a link that opens the page and follows its name if you rename it. |
| **Delete** | Asks first, then deletes the page. |

## Delete a page

1. Choose **Delete** in the row's menu, or **Delete page…** at the end of the page's **Page options** menu.
2. A dialog asks **Delete "Draft"?** and says the page and everything in it will be removed.
3. Press <kbd>Return</kbd> or click **Delete** to confirm. <kbd>Esc</kbd> or **Cancel** keeps the page.

After a delete, the next page in the list opens. If it was your last page, the main area shows **No page open**.

Links to a deleted page from other pages read **Deleted page**, struck through. See [Links and backlinks](/organize/links).

::: info Limits
There is no trash view yet. A deleted page is removed from your lists at once.
:::

## Thoughts and essays under a page

A page's sidenotes are kept on its **Thoughts** page, and an essay you start from it is a page of its own. Both belong to their page, so the sidebar doesn't list them at the top level.

When you open the page, its Thoughts page and essay show under its row as a small tree. The branch to the one you are on is drawn in the theme's main colour. Open another page and the tree folds away. Their row menus offer only **Copy link** and **Delete**.

If you delete the page itself, its Thoughts page and essay stay, and are listed at the top level from then on.

To start an essay, open **Page options** and choose **Start an essay on this page**. Once there is one, the item reads **Open essay**. See [Sidenotes, thoughts and essays](/write/sidenotes).

## Page options

The **Page options** button ("…") sits in the page's property bar, the line under the title with the **Type** picker and **Add property**. It holds the page's own actions:

- **Publish page…** shares a read-only copy, including sidenotes, by an unlisted link. See [Publish a page](/organize/publishing).
- **Save as type…** turns the page's properties into a new type. See [Types and templates](/organize/types).
- **Start an essay on this page** (or **Open essay**).
- **To-do and highlight style**, with **Plain** and **Playful**. See below.
- **Delete page…**, in red.

To open the list of all pages of this type, click the type's name in the card header.

## Page styles: Plain and Playful

Each page has a style. It changes how to-dos and highlights look, so a page with neither looks the same in both.

| Style | Checked to-dos | Highlights |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Plain** (the default) | A filled box with a check. The words are muted and struck through with a straight line. No sound. | A plain highlight. |
| **Playful** | An open box with a hand-drawn check and a hand-drawn line through the words. A to-do you check now is drawn off like a pen, with a soft tap. | A marker stroke. |

To change it, open **Page options** and pick **Plain** or **Playful** under **To-do and highlight style**. The menu closes and nothing on the page moves. Each page keeps its own style.

::: info Limits
Task lines on a Playful page still show a plain box and a straight line, not the pen marks.
:::
