# The capture inbox

What you capture in a hurry can wait until you are ready to sort it out. **Captures** collects every
idea, saved webpage and voice recording from [Quick capture](/capture/quick-capture) in one list, and
lets you mark each one reviewed when you have dealt with it.

The inbox doesn't copy anything. Each item is the page itself, and reviewing it never moves or deletes
the page.

## Open Captures

Click **Captures** in [the bar](/workspace/bar) at the right edge of the window, or press
<kbd>⌥⌘K</kbd>. Press it again to close the panel.

A number on the Captures icon shows how many captures wait to be reviewed. There is no row for the
inbox in the sidebar.

## What is listed

Captures has two tabs: **To review** and **Reviewed**. Each lists captures newest first. Under each
title the row shows its kind and the date you captured it:

| Kind | Where it came from |
| --- | --- |
| **Captured idea** | Words typed in the capture box |
| **Saved webpage** | An address saved from the capture box. See [Saving web pages](/capture/web-sources). |
| **Voice recording** | A recording made in the capture box. See [Voice notes](/capture/voice). |

Only captures are listed. Ordinary pages, and ideas you made yourself rather than in the capture box,
are not. Ideas from earlier versions of LeadingLight that are tagged in-the-middle, and webpages saved
before the inbox existed, are listed too. The first time you open Captures, LeadingLight looks for older
recordings, and shows **Looking for saved recordings…** meanwhile.

Click a capture to open its page beside the list, with its body and audio as they were. A long list has
a **Show more** button at the end.

## Sort out a capture without opening it

Each row has its own controls:

- **Project**: pick a project for the capture, or **No project**. A saved webpage can go in a project
  too.
- **Add tag**: add a tag to the capture. Its tags show beside the button, and you can remove one there.

These change the page itself: its other properties and its body stay as they were, and your choices are
still there after a reload. If the page is open beside the list, it shows the change at once.

## Mark captures reviewed

When you have dealt with a capture, press **Mark reviewed** on its row (in the panel, the check mark).
It leaves **To review** and shows under **Reviewed**. Its page doesn't change, and an idea stays among your Ideas.

A note at the top of the list says **Marked reviewed** with the capture's title, and an **Undo** button.
Press **Undo** to put it back in To review.

To send a reviewed capture back, open **Reviewed** and press **Return to inbox** on its row (in the
panel, the arrow). The note then says **Returned to inbox**, with its own **Undo**.

Review at your own pace: what you mark stays marked after a reload.

### A new recording brings a page back

If you save a new voice recording on a page you already reviewed, that page goes back to **To review**,
with the time of the new recording. No second page is made.

A recording whose transcription failed also waits in To review, so you can come back to it.

## When nothing is waiting

With nothing to review, **To review** says **Nothing waiting here**, and that ideas, webpages and
recordings from Quick capture appear here.

With nothing reviewed yet, **Reviewed** says **No reviewed captures yet**.

## When a change can't be saved

If marking a capture reviewed can't be saved, the capture stays in **To review** and its row explains
what went wrong. Try again; nothing in the page is lost.

If older recordings can't be loaded, Captures says so and offers **Retry loading**.

## Captures and the pages you are writing

You can keep a capture open and type in it while the inbox is beside it. If you mark it reviewed or
change its project from the inbox meanwhile, both your latest words and the change are kept.
