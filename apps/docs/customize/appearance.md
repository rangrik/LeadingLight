# Appearance and themes

LeadingLight follows your Mac's light and dark appearance by default. In **Settings › Appearance** you can fix it to one, pick a theme that colours the whole app, and set how large the text is and how wide pages run.

To open it, press <kbd>⌘,</kbd> and choose **Appearance**. It is the first section, so Settings opens on it.

## Choose light, dark or Match system

At the top, **Light or dark** offers three choices:

<img class="shot" src="/screenshots/dark.webp" alt="A page in LeadingLight Dark" />

| Choice | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Light** | Always uses a light theme. |
| **Dark** | Always uses a dark theme. |
| **Match system** | Follows your Mac: a light theme by day, a dark one when the Mac is in dark mode. This is the default. |

Everything follows the choice at once: the page, the sidebar, menus, toolbars and the editor.

If the theme you are using is already light and you choose **Light** (or dark and you choose **Dark**), it stays. Otherwise LeadingLight switches to the light or dark theme that **Match system** would use.

### Pick the pair Match system uses

While **Match system** is chosen, a row called **By day, and when it's dark** shows two menus: the theme to use in light mode and the theme to use in dark mode. You can pair any light theme with any dark one. Unless you change them, they are **LeadingLight Light** and **LeadingLight Dark**.

The note under **Light or dark** says which two themes are in use, and in the theme list the one showing right now is marked **Now**.

## Pick a theme

Below the choice is the list of themes, each drawn as a small picture of the app in its own colours. Click one to use it. Its card shows a check, and the whole app takes its colours: the sidebar, the page, the editor's text and the colours of code in code blocks. Your choice is kept when you reopen the app.

<img class="shot" src="/screenshots/settings-appearance.webp" alt="Settings › Appearance with the theme gallery" />

Picking a theme by hand leaves **Match system**. To follow your Mac again, choose **Match system**.

The themes come in three groups:

- **LeadingLight**: the app's own four themes.
- **Light** and **Dark**: themes from popular code editors. Each group shows four at first, plus the one you use. The line below the list, such as **Show 11 more**, names the hidden ones; click it to see them all, and **Show fewer** to fold them away again.

### Find a theme

Type part of a name into **Find a theme**, at the top right of the list. Only matching themes are listed. For example, "nord" leaves only **Nord**.

## Every theme stays readable

Each built-in theme is checked before it ships, so text and quieter grey text keep enough contrast to read on every surface. Dark themes are checked in the editor too: selected blocks stand clearly apart from the page and their text stays readable, and an unticked to-do box is plainly an outlined box.

::: info Limits
In a few themes the shell is less clear than it should be. In **Nord** the sidebar and the page are the same colour, so the page's edge is hard to see. In **Solarized Light** the selected sidebar row barely shows. In dark themes an off switch or an unticked checkbox in Settings can look faint.
:::

## Change the text size

Under **Interface**, **Text size** offers **Small**, **Default** and **Large**. The size applies to the whole interface: the sidebar, Settings, the page title and the text you write.

You can also step it from the keyboard, anywhere in the app:

| Keys | Menu item | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <kbd>⌘+</kbd> | **View › Zoom in** | Makes text one step larger. |
| <kbd>⌘−</kbd> | **View › Zoom out** | Makes text one step smaller. |
| <kbd>⌘0</kbd> | **View › Actual size** | Puts text back to the default size. |

If you zoom to a size that is not one of the three, it shows as an extra choice beside them, such as **15px**.

## Set the content width

**Content width** sets the widest a page's text can run, in pixels. Type a number, or use the − and + buttons beside it. Any width from 320 to 1600 is allowed; a number outside that range is brought back to the nearest end.

- Press <kbd>Return</kbd> to keep the new width.
- Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> to keep it and close Settings in one go.

The width applies to every open page. The title, properties and text line up to it, and the block handles and sidenotes keep their room at the sides. It is kept when you reopen the app.

### Use the full width

Turn on **Full width** to let pages use all the room in their pane. **Content width** is greyed out while it is on. Turn it off and your earlier width comes back.

A narrow pane always fits the text to the space it has, so you never need to scroll sideways, whatever width you set.
