# Settings reference

Settings is where you change how LeadingLight looks and works. This page walks through each section in the order Settings lists them, with a link to the page that explains it in full.

## Open and close Settings

Open Settings in any of these ways:

- Press <kbd>⌘,</kbd>.
- Choose **Go › Settings…** in the Mac app's menu bar.
- Click **Settings** at the bottom of the sidebar.

Settings takes the place of your pages. Its sidebar lists the sections, under **Back to app**, and the chosen section fills the panel. It opens on **Appearance**. <kbd>⌘&#92;</kbd> folds and unfolds its sidebar, as it does on a page.

To go back to your pages, press <kbd>Esc</kbd> or click **Back to app**. You return to the page that was open.

- <kbd>Esc</kbd> with a menu open closes only the menu. Press it again to close Settings.
- <kbd>Esc</kbd> while you are typing in a Settings text field leaves Settings open.
- <kbd>⌘N</kbd> closes Settings and makes a new page, with the title ready to type.

While Settings is open, the pages underneath can't be clicked or typed into, and nothing you type in Settings reaches a page. A page's unsaved words are kept, and you carry on where you left off when you go back.

## Appearance

How LeadingLight looks.

- **Theme**: **Light or dark** (**Light**, **Dark** or **Match system**), the two themes **Match system** switches between, and the list of themes with **Find a theme**.
- **Interface**: **Full width**, **Content width** and **Text size**.

See [Appearance and themes](/customize/appearance).

## Writing

The writing tools and which pages they work on.

- **Tools**: a switch for each tool, with its key beside it.
  - **Focus mode**, with **Focus on**: **Sentence**, **Paragraph** or **Typewriter**.
  - **Syntax highlight**, with the **Parts of speech** to colour.
  - **Style check**, with what to **Strike through**: **Fillers**, **Redundancies** and **Clichés**.
  - **Grammar check**, and **Grammar help**: **When I ask** or **While I write**.
  - **Counts**.
- **Types of pages**: **Use the tools on** lists your types with a checkbox each. The suggestion is on for pages you write in and off for records such as people and books. Once you change it, **Use suggestions** puts the suggestion back.

See [Writing tools](/write/writing-tools) and [Writing coach](/write/writing-coach).

## Types

Every type in your space, in the sidebar's order, each with how many pages it has. **Edit** opens a type's settings: its name, icon, colour, properties, layout and templates. **New type** makes one.

See [Types and templates](/organize/types).

## Properties

Every property, oldest first. Each row shows the property's kind and the types that use it, or **Not in a type**. **Edit** changes one, and **New property** makes one. Properties are shared by the whole space, so a change shows on every page that has it.

See [Properties](/organize/properties).

## Objects

Everything LeadingLight keeps for you and its agents, in one list: projects, tasks, agent profiles, reports, questions and the rest. Each says what it is for, who makes it, what can change and where it shows. Where there are pages of that kind, **Show** opens their list. A second group, **On a task and in the Feed**, describes the parts a task shows, such as its activity, checklist and review.

See [Objects, types and links](/guide/concepts) and [Assigning tasks to agents](/ai/tasks-for-agents).

## Capture

- **New idea shortcut**: the key that opens the capture box, <kbd>⌃⌥I</kbd> unless you change it. In the Mac app it works over any app; in a browser, only in LeadingLight's window. Click the key to change it, as in **Shortcuts**.
- **Open at login** <Badge type="info" text="Mac app" />: starts LeadingLight when you log in, so the shortcut works after a restart.

See [Idea capture](/capture/idea-capture).

## Browser

The built-in browser keeps a profile of its own for your pages and agents. This section is not shown on iPad.

- **Downloads** <Badge type="info" text="Mac app" />: **Download location**, with **Choose folder…** and **Reset to Downloads**, and **Ask where to save downloads**. In a browser these are greyed out, and your browser's own download settings apply.
- **Developer tools**: **Allow page developer access** lets you ask to inspect a page's layout, network and performance. Each page still needs your approval, and turning it off takes back every approval.
- **Saved feedback**: how many browser comments are saved, and **Remove saved comments…** to clear them from every workspace. Comments already attached to a chat stay there.
- **Personal browser history**: the pages you visited, with **Search history**, **Remove** for one page and **Clear browsing data…**. Project browsers keep their own history in the browser's menu.

See [The built-in browser](/ai/browser).

## AI

Claude through Claude Code, and GPT through Codex, both on this Mac. There is no key to enter.

- **Account**: whether **Claude Code** and **Codex** are installed and signed in, with **Check again**.
- **Command-line tools**: the version of each tool found on this Mac, **Update** when a newer one is out, and **Check for updates** to look for them in the background.
- **Projects**: **Linked folders and project chats** turns on linking a page to a folder on this Mac. **Runtime** shows whether the project runtime is running, with **Start** or **Restart**.
- **Answers**: the **Model** and **Effort** to use, and **Access**: **Ask for approval**, **Approve for me** or **Full access**.
- **Tail**: **Offers** (**Off**, **Quiet** or **Normal**), how many seconds to wait before it reads a block, its own **Model** and **Effort**, the types it **Reads on**, **How you work** (notes tail keeps about you, which you can edit or delete) and when it last ran.
- **Agents**: the agents you can mention with `@`, each a Claude Code agent file. **Show folder** opens the folder where yours go.
- **Privacy**: **What leaves this Mac**, in plain words.

On iPad this section only explains that agents run in LeadingLight on your Mac.

See [AI overview and setup](/ai/), [Ask Claude about a page](/ai/ask), [Tail and suggestions](/ai/tail) and [Projects linked to folders](/ai/projects).

## Shortcuts

Every app shortcut, grouped under **File**, **View**, **Go** and **Help**, with **Search shortcuts** at the top. Click a shortcut's keys to change them; the reset button beside a changed one puts the default back. In the Mac app they are in the menu bar too.

See [Keyboard shortcuts](/customize/shortcuts).

## About

Which build of LeadingLight this is:

- the version, under the title
- **Build**: the commit the app was built from. **+dirty** means it was built with changes that were not yet committed.
- **Built**: the date and time it was built

The same commit shows beside **About** in the Settings sidebar, so you can tell builds apart at a glance. **Built on** names the open-source project LeadingLight's themes, Settings and agent code come from, with a link to its source, and links to the notices for the themes that began elsewhere.

When you report a problem, include what **About** says.

## Account and sync

Your account is not a section of Settings. Click **Account and sync** in the sidebar, just above **Settings**, to open **Your workspace, everywhere**. It has three tabs:

- **Account**: sign in to connect another device, or sign out. You can keep writing without an account.
- **Sync**: set up encrypted sync, connect a device with a recovery key, **Sync now**, show your recovery key, or pause sync on this device.
- **Agents**: connect a browser to the agents on your Mac. Not shown on iPad.

See [Accounts and encrypted sync](/sync/) and [Agents from a browser](/sync/pairing).
