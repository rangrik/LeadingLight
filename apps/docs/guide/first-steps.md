# Your first ten minutes

This tour walks through the parts of LeadingLight you will use every day. Each step takes a minute and links to the page that explains it in full.

## 1. Write a page

1. Click **New page** in the sidebar, or press <kbd>⌘N</kbd>.
2. Type a title, such as *Trip to Kyoto*, and press <kbd>Return</kbd>. The cursor moves to the body.
3. Type `# ` (a hash and a space) to start a heading, then *Before we go*.
4. Type `[] ` to start a to-do, `- ` for a bullet list, or `> ` for a quote.

There is no save button. LeadingLight saves as you type, and <kbd>⌘S</kbd> saves at once if you want to be sure.

<img class="shot" src="/screenshots/editor.webp" alt="A page with a heading, to-dos, a quote, a bullet list and a link to a book" />

→ [The block editor](/write/editor)

## 2. Insert anything with /

Type `/` on an empty line. The slash menu lists every kind of block: text, headings, lists, toggles, quotes, code, tables, columns, images, video, files, embeds and more. Keep typing to filter it, then press <kbd>Return</kbd>.

<img class="shot" src="/screenshots/slash-menu.webp" alt="The slash menu open on an empty line, listing block kinds in sections" />

→ [Block kinds](/write/blocks)

## 3. Format and move

- Select words to see the formatting toolbar: bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, code, highlight and link.
- Hover a block to see its handle at the left. Drag the handle to move the block. Click it for the block menu: **Turn into**, **Duplicate** and **Delete**.

<img class="shot" src="/screenshots/block-menu.webp" alt="The block menu beside a to-do, with Turn into open and a list of block kinds" />

→ [Selecting and moving blocks](/write/selecting-blocks)

## 4. Make a book, and link to it

1. In the sidebar under **Types**, click **Books**, then **New book**. Call it *Dune*.
2. A Book comes with properties: **Author**, **Rating**, **Status**, **Finished on** and **Tags**. Click a value to set it. For **Author**, type a name and choose **Create person** to make a Person page at the same time.
3. Go back to *Trip to Kyoto*. Type `@Dun` and choose *Dune* from the menu. The link shows the book's icon and follows it if you rename the book.

<img class="shot" src="/screenshots/mention-menu.webp" alt="Typing @Dun opens a menu offering the book Dune, or creating a new page of any type" />

→ [Properties](/organize/properties) · [Links and backlinks](/organize/links)

## 5. See the backlink

Open *Dune*. At the end of the page, **Backlinks** lists *Trip to Kyoto* with the line that mentions it. You never have to link both ways by hand.

<img class="shot" src="/screenshots/backlinks.webp" alt="The Dune page with a Backlinks section listing Trip to Kyoto and its line" />

## 6. Give it a face

Hover the title to show **Add icon**, **Add cover** and **Add description**. An icon can be any emoji, an icon in a colour, or an image. A cover can be a colour, an upload, a link or an Unsplash photo.

→ [Icons, covers and descriptions](/organize/page-header)

## 7. See all your books

Click **Books** in the sidebar. Switch between the list, table, gallery and board views at the top, and choose how to sort and group them. A board grouped by **Status** shows what you're reading at a glance.

<img class="shot" src="/screenshots/view-board.webp" alt="The Books type as a board grouped by status: To read, Reading and Read" />

→ [Lists, tables and boards](/organize/views)

## 8. Open two pages side by side

Shift-click a page in the sidebar to open it beside the one you're on, or drag a page to the edge of the window. Each side keeps its own tabs.

<img class="shot" src="/screenshots/panes.webp" alt="Trip to Kyoto and Dune open side by side" />

→ [Sidebar, panes and tabs](/workspace/layout)

## 9. Capture an idea

Press <kbd>⌃⌥I</kbd>. A small box opens; type what's on your mind, then press <kbd>⌘↩</kbd> to save and close. In the Mac app this works from any app, without switching to LeadingLight. The idea is saved as an Idea page and shows in **Today**.

<img class="shot" src="/screenshots/capture-box.webp" alt="The idea capture box over a page, with the idea typed and Saved shown" />

→ [Idea capture](/capture/idea-capture) · [Tags and daily notes](/organize/tags-daily)

## 10. Ask about it

In the Mac app, if you use Claude Code or Codex, select some words and press <kbd>⌘J</kbd> to ask about them, or open **Ask** on any page. Claude can read the page and, when you approve, change it.

→ [AI and agents](/ai/)
