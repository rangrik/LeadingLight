# Writing coach

The writing coach helps you finish a piece of writing without breaking your train of thought. It stays quiet while you draft. When you are ready, it shows one suggestion at a time, and you choose what to change. Learning is optional, and everything stays on your Mac.

A correction is a choice, not a judgment. The coach never rewrites your words by itself, never changes your tone, and never gives you a score.

## Turn it on

The coach is part of **Grammar check**, which is on to begin with. Check it in **Settings › Writing**:

- **Grammar check** turns checking on or off (<kbd>⇧⌘G</kbd>).
- **Grammar help** chooses how it shows up:
  - **When I ask** (the default): no underlines, no totals and no pop-ups while you write. You review when you choose to.
  - **While I write**: mistakes are underlined as you write. Click an underline to open its suggestion.

Showing word counts never starts a grammar check by itself in **When I ask**.

Grammar check works on the types of pages chosen under **Use the tools on**. See [Writing tools](/write/writing-tools#choose-which-pages-the-tools-work-on).

## Review your writing

1. Open the **Writing** menu at the right of the page's header.
2. Choose **Review writing**.

The **Review writing** window opens on its **Review** tab. It says what it is checking: **Whole page**, or **Selected text** if you selected words first. It shows one suggestion:

- the sentence around it, with the words in question marked,
- **Your words**, the words as you wrote them,
- **Suggested**, the proposed change. When there is more than one, pick it from **Suggestion**.

You can do all of this from the keyboard.

### Review only part of a page

Select a passage before you choose **Review writing**. Only corrections fully inside the selection are offered; a word you selected only part of is left out. Applying and undoing never touch the rest of the page. Clear the selection and review again to check the whole page.

## Apply or keep

- **Apply** changes only those words. Their formatting, such as bold, stays. The change is its own undo step, separate from your typing before and after it.
- **Keep this wording** leaves your words as they are. That one place leaves the review for now and loses its underline; the same wording elsewhere is still offered.

After **Apply**, the coach pauses. It shows the change you made, with **Undo**, and waits. The next suggestion only appears when you choose **Next suggestion**. You can stop here; closing is always enough to get back to writing.

**Undo** puts your words back so you can look again. If the page changes elsewhere in the meantime, **Undo** goes away rather than undo someone else's edit.

Choose **Done for now** to close the review. Your words stay as they are.

### When the page changes during a review

If the words change before you apply a suggestion, the old suggestion can't change the new text. The review says **The words changed. Review the updated suggestion.** and checks again.

A suggestion on screen stays put while other paragraphs finish checking. A quick double-click on **Apply** or **Keep this wording** acts only once.

## Learn why

Choose **Why?** under a suggestion to see a short explanation. It still works right after you apply a change, and explains the change you just made.

For some common slips, the coach has a short lesson with one example:

- **A or an**
- **Match the subject and verb**
- **Notice repeated words**
- **Could have, not could of**

A lesson offers:

- **Try one**: a two-choice practice question. Either answer explains the choice, with no score or "wrong". Practice never edits your page, and you can move on without answering.
- **Save tip**: keeps the lesson to read later.

For other suggestions, **Why?** shows the checker's own explanation.

## Come back to saved tips

Open the **Saved tips** tab in the review to reread a tip or try its practice again. Choose **Remove saved tip** to remove one.

Saved tips hold the general lesson and its example, never your own sentences or a history of your mistakes. There are no streaks, reminders or claims that you have mastered something.

## Keep your own words

Names, terms from your field and other words you use often can be flagged as spelling mistakes. On a spelling suggestion, choose **Remember word**. The word stays as it is and is no longer flagged, also after you restart.

The **My words** tab lists the words you chose to keep. They are saved on this device. Click **Remove** beside a word to have it checked again.

If a word can't be saved, the review says so and your words stay unchanged. Try **Remember word** again.

## Listen

Choose **Listen** to hear the suggestion, or a tip, read aloud. It speaks only when you ask, using an English voice that is installed on your Mac; it never uses an online voice. Choose **Stop listening**, or close the review, to stop.

If your Mac has no local English voice, the review says **Listen needs a local English system voice.**

## What is never checked

- Code blocks and inline code.
- Links to pages.
- Words quoted into an essay from a source. See [Sidenotes, thoughts and essays](/write/sidenotes).
- Read-only pages, such as an agent's report.

## When checking is slow or fails

The checker runs on your Mac and can take a moment on a long page. While it works, the review says **Checking on this device…**. It never says there is nothing to fix before it has finished.

If the checker can't finish, the review says **The local checker could not finish. Your words are unchanged.** Choose **Retry** to check again, without reloading.

When a check finds nothing, the review says **No suggestions from this check. You can keep writing.**

::: info Limits
The coach checks English only. It has four short lessons so far; other suggestions use the checker's explanation. **Keep this wording** lasts while the page is open, not for good.
:::
