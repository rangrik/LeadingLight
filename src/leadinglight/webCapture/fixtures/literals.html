Notes on Markdown Syntax

This page talks about note syntax, so its text is full of characters that look like markup. Every one of them is meant to be read literally by the person who saved the page, exactly as it appears here.

Wikilinks look like [[Private note]] and [[Folder/Note|alias]]. Highlights look like ==important== or ==red==warning==. Inline math looks like $a$ and $b$, and prices like $5 or $10 are not math.

A callout starts with [!note] and a footnote with [^1]. HTML such as <script>alert(1)</script> and <img src=x onerror=alert(1)> is only text here. Entities like &amp; &#x3c; &#60; &copy; and AT&T stay as typed.

Fences: ``` and ~~~ and `code` and **bold** and __under__ and ~strike~ and *star* are literal.
# not a heading
> not a quote
- not a list
1. not ordered
| not | a | table |
--- not a rule
===

Across elements: a==b, [[x]], $a$, C:\*x, wow!link after bang, x==y and == alone.
